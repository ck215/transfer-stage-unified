XYZ Mega Wiring

One Arduino Mega 2560 and three BIGTREETECH TMC2209 V1.2 drivers (X, Y, Z) on one shared UART bus, driving the 50 mm XYZ stage through three DB9 connectors. The pin map is MEGA_STANDARD section 5; this sheet is that map drawn out. Driver pins use the same J1/J2 numbering as the one-axis Teensy sheet (stepper_validator/wiring.html), counted from the potentiometer end.

  • Never plug or unplug a DB9, a coil wire or a driver with VM powered. Wait about 5 s after switching off for the capacitors to drain.
  • Power order: USB first, then VM. Off: VM first, then USB.
  • Set the address straps (MS1/MS2) before power. The driver reads them at power-up only.
  • 100 µF goes on VM and GND only, at each driver, never on a signal line. Electrolytic: the long, unmarked lead to VM, the striped lead to GND.
  • Reversed VM destroys the driver. Meter the PSU leads before they touch a module.

1. Control lines: Mega to each driver

2. Shared UART junction, motor supply and common ground

Common ground: one rail joins the PSU minus, all three drivers (J2-2 and J2-8, which are tied on the module), each stage's DB9 pin 4 and the Mega GND. Meter once with everything off: the PSU minus to the Mega GND, and to every J2-8, each about 0 Ω.

Pin 4 vs pin 5 caveat: the stage's own sheet contradicts itself on whether the limit switches return to pin 4 or pin 5. This sheet follows the one-axis sheet: both switches return to pin 4 (GND). Meter it before trusting the limits: resistance 2 to 4 (LS1) and 3 to 4 (LS2), with the carriage at centre and then on that switch. The reading must change. If 2-4 never changes, test 2-5 and stop to report it: with a pin-5 return a switch only reads while the home slot is clear. Under normally-open contacts the Mega reads a switch only when its other side reaches GND.
Motor (42BYGH613-01B), if you wire the motor directly: black and green are coil A, red and blue are coil B (datasheet). Coil A goes on J2-3/J2-4 and coil B on J2-5/J2-6; swapping the two wires of one coil only reverses that axis. Never mix an A lead with a B lead on one driver pair. Each coil reads about 1.5 Ω, and the two coils are open to each other.

Parts

  • Arduino Mega 2560 and a USB cable.
  • Three BIGTREETECH TMC2209 V1.2 (or V1.3) modules.
  • One 1 kΩ resistor in series with TX2.
  • Three 100 µF electrolytics, rated at least 25 V (35 V for a 24 V supply).
  • Motor supply: 12 V DC, sized for three axes (at least 3 A; the bench supply is set to 12.0 V, then check each axis current), red and black leads, 22 AWG or heavier.
  • Three DB9 breakouts, one per stage axis. A multimeter.

Meter checks before power

USB unplugged, VM off, PSU off, stages not yet plugged into the drivers.

  • Straps: read each module before power. X: MS1 and MS2 both to GND. Y: MS1 to VIO, MS2 to GND. Z: MS1 to GND, MS2 to VIO. Meter each strap to J2-8 (GND) and to J2-7 (VIO).
  • Junction: the three J1-4 pins read about 0 Ω to each other and to RX2 (17); TX2 (16) to the junction reads about 1 kΩ.
  • Power polarity: PSU + (red) on VM, PSU − (black) on GND, metered at the leads before they touch the modules. Each capacitor: stripe on GND.
  • No short: VM to GND on each driver is not 0 Ω (a capacitor charging is fine).
  • Common ground: PSU − to Mega GND, and to J2-8 of each driver, about 0 Ω.
  • VIO: J2-7 is on the Mega 5V line, not on VM and not on 3.3 V.
  • Each DB9 on its breakout: coils 6-7 and 8-9 read about 1.5-2.5 Ω, 6 or 7 to 8 or 9 open, any coil pin to 4 open. Photo-interrupter LED on diode test, red on 1, black on 4: about 1.1-1.4 V, OL reversed. Limit switches 2-4 and 3-4 change with the carriage on the switch (see the pin 4 vs pin 5 caveat).

Bring-up order

  1. Everything off. Wire it all, straps first.
  2. Meter checks (above).
  3. Connect each DB9 to its axis with VM off, carriages about 25 mm from each end.
  4. USB in, VM off. The address straps were set before power, so after VM comes on and the firmware reads the drivers, #INFO must show x_tmc=y_tmc=z_tmc=1. If an axis shows 0, a strap or the PDN_UART junction is wrong; power down and re-meter.
  5. VM on (the TMC2209 logic runs from VM, so UART replies only appear once VM is up), then check #INFO.
  6. Shut down: disable, VM off, wait about 5 s, then USB.
