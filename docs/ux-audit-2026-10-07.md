# Web view UX audit, 2026-10-07

Scope: the flows that changed today, end to end: the sign-in screen (sign in,
create account, proceed as guest), the full-screen Setup (scan, device table,
Hard reset, launch), the device pages and the rail's status dots, the
per-device tutorials, the Transfer Map and the Sample Map, the event tray,
the stopped (latched) state and a device in error. The question was the
operator's: a comfortable, good-looking workspace for long sessions at the
bench (a 1080p or larger monitor, read from across the bench, gloves or a
mouse, a gamepad in hand).

Method: a SIM station built in-process (the tests' way: a real `Controller`
and a real `Setup`), with every route to hardware closed: `pyserial.Serial`
replaced by a class that raises, the port listing returning only invented
paths (`/dev/ttyACM90`, `/dev/ttyACM91`, `/dev/ttyUSB90`), `Setup.identify`
replaced (it never opens anything), the gamepad hub listing nothing, flashing
disabled. Neither `src/app.py` nor `run.sh` was run, and port 8080 was never
used. The fake Stepper Probe "found" on `/dev/ttyACM90` fails to open, which
supplies the device-in-error state. Headless Chrome (puppeteer) drove the
page as an operator would; the WCAG ratios were computed from
`src/palette.py` through `theme.mix()`.

Screenshots: 142 captures at 1920x1080 (`-L`), 1366x768 (`-M`) and 390x844
(`-S`), plus the Setup table at 768, 1024 and 1366 wide, before and after.
They are scratch files, not in the repository; each name below is
`before-NN-...` or `after-NN-...` in the audit's scratch folder
(`uxaudit/shots/`). Only one theme exists: the palette is a single light
set (`color-scheme: light`), so there is no dark capture.

## Findings, by impact

Each finding says what it is, where it shows, and whether this branch fixes
it (**Fixed**) or proposes something (**Proposal**, see the last section).

### 1. A class-name collision from today's merge restyled every "why it cannot go" line (Fixed)

The sign-in screen's guest notice uses `.gate-note`, and the sign-in commit
(948547f) added an unscoped `.gate-note { font-size: var(--t-md); max-width: 36ch; }`.
`.gate-note` is also L3's line under a row whose go command cannot go ("Link
lost: press Stop, check the cable, then relaunch from Setup", "Nothing to
launch yet"). Every one of those went from a 13 px muted caption across the
row to body size in a 36-character column, floating mid-row in Setup's
Launch line (`before-04-setup-scanning-L`, `before-10-page-stepper-probe-L`).
The rule is now scoped to `.gate .gate-note`; the guest notice also runs the
form's full width instead of a narrower column of its own.

### 2. On a phone, every layer moved off screen once the rail grew taller than wide (Fixed)

`reserveLogSpace()` decided "column or bar" by `height > width`. On a phone
the rail is a bar across the top, but with a stop line, a lost-device line,
the energized caution and the page list wrapped in it, the bar is 506 px tall
on a 390 px screen, so it was taken for a column: `--rail-left` became 390 px
and Setup, the tray, the dialogs and the sign-in screen were all placed one
screen width to the right (`before-13-setup-phone`: the drawer at x=390,
1 px wide). The stop itself stayed reachable; everything else was not. The
client now asks the stylesheet which shape the rail has
(`flexDirection === 'column'`). After: the drawer sits under the bar
(`after-13-setup-phone`).

### 3. The Setup device table broke under about 1000 px wide (Fixed)

The two parallel edits to the table's tracks left the name and status
columns at `minmax(0, max-content)`, so a narrow drawer shrank them to
nothing: "Stepper", "DC Prob", "Chuck P", "Tempera" under the Port
dropdowns, the Gamepad dropdown painted over "Simulated", and "Detected:
Stepper Probe" broken into four lines mid-word (`before-12-setup-table-768`;
the overlap checker found it at 768 and 390). Now the name and status tracks
never shrink below their longest word and wrap at spaces, and under 1000 px
(where the rail narrows to 200 px and the drawer has under 560 px) the table
takes the phone's stacked shape: the name on its own line, the port, Hard
reset, the gamepad and the status under it (`after-12-setup-table-768`).
The overlap checker is clean at 390, 600, 768, 900, 1024, 1280, 1366, 1600,
1920 and 2560 px.

### 4. Setup's sections each placed their keys somewhere different, and the table jumped at every scan (Fixed)

In the full-screen Setup, Account's keys sat alone on a second line at the
far right (a `.actions:last-child` rule written for the Launch row matched
only Account), Update's sat under the text at the left, Firmware's wherever
the text ended (`before-05-setup-ready-L`). While a scan ran, its long
status ("Scanning /dev/ttyACM90 (1 of 3), 2 s on this port. Launch waits
for the scan; press Cancel scan to launch now.") pushed Address onto a line
of its own, so the whole device table dropped about 50 px and came back up
when the scan ended, under the operator's pointer. Now every section reads
the same way: the name, what it says (wrapping in the room it has), and its
keys at the right edge (Devices keeps Refresh beside its name). The scan
line wraps in place, so nothing below it moves (`after-04-setup-scanning-L`,
`after-05-setup-ready-L`). Wrapped Setup values got a readable line height.

### 5. The status dots are colour-only in practice, and the stop state is drawn twice (Proposal)

The dots are 10 px. Their three colours are nearly the same lightness:
grey/blue 1.13:1, blue/red 1.35:1, grey/red 1.20:1, so an operator with a
red-green deficiency, or anyone at three metres, sees three identical dark
dots. Only the error dot has a 2 px halo. There is no legend. On a latched
station every dot turns red beside the lamp that already says "stopped", and
the Stepper Probe's row carried three marks before its name (lamp, energized
ring, dot) (`before-21-stopped-L`). The words are there for a screen reader
(aria-label and title), so this is a visual problem only. The colours are an
owner ruling and are pinned by tests (`.nav-dot` background is
`var(--muted)` / `var(--trace)` / `var(--signal)`), so they are not changed
here.

### 6. The Overview's entry head painted the state over the name (Fixed)

At 1366 px, a three-across Overview entry drew "Connection lost" on top of
"Stepper Probe": the name's track could shrink to zero and the state was
`nowrap`, right-aligned, so it overflowed leftwards (`before-08-after-launch-M`).
The name now keeps at least its longest word, and an Overview entry narrower
than 32rem (a container query) puts its state on a line of its own under the
name, with "Open" kept beside the name (`after3-10-page-overview-M`; wide
entries are unchanged, `after3-10-page-overview-L`).

### 7. The heaviest key in Setup is the one that overwrites firmware (Proposal)

"Update now" and "Flash out-of-date boards" are ink `go` keys, the page's
primary emphasis, even when the update check is off and no board has been
checked (`before-05-setup-ready-L`). Flashing overwrites a board's running
firmware; it should not be the thing the eye lands on in a Setup with
nothing pending. The role comes from Setup's schema (`src/controller/setup.py`),
which is the server's side, so it is not changed here.

### 8. The Rotator offers SIM but has no simulator, which raises false alarms (Fixed c56cf76)

Setup lets the Rotator row be set to SIM and then says "Simulated"
(`before-06-setup-sim-L`). The model says, correctly, that "a SIM or absent
port is not a simulated rotator: it is no rotator", so after launch the
Rotator shows "Stale" and "Not connected", its dot is red, the rail's
"Simulated:" line leaves it out, and every Stop reports "Stepper Probe and
Rotator did not confirm" (`before-10-page-rotator-L`, `before-21-stopped-L`).
A red that is always there teaches an operator to ignore red. Setup should
not offer SIM for a model without a simulator, or should say "No simulator:
not launched" in the row.

### 9. The sign-in screen's refusal had no mark and moved the keys under the pointer (Fixed)

A refusal ("No account for nobody@lab.test on this station. Press Create
account... to make one, or Proceed as guest.") appeared as plain ink with no
mark, and pushed Sign in and Create account down by a line just as the
operator reached for them (`before-02-gate-error-M`). The line now keeps its
room while empty and carries the warning glyph in ink (a refused sign-in is
not a stop, so not in signal) (`after-02-gate-error-L`).

### 10. Typography for reading across the bench (partly Fixed)

The page list on the rail was 14 px, the size of a caption, and Setup's
column captions 12/400 muted, the smallest text on the page
(`before-05-setup-ready-L`). The page list is now 15 px and the column
captions 13/500 engraved, like every other caption. The readings themselves
(60 px focal, 44 px primary) already read from across the bench; most of
the rest of the sheet is 13 to 15 px. The `--font-size` launch flag scales
the whole page, and is the knob for a bench monitor read from a distance.

### 11. A unit after a word (Fixed)

The Temperature Controller on SIM read "Current temperature: Simulated °C"
(`before-10-page-temperature-controller-L`). A unit now shows only beside a
number or "--" (unknown, in degrees); beside a word it is hidden.

### 12. Short and narrow screens: the rail takes the room (Proposal)

At 1366x768 with a stop line, a lost device and the energized caution, the
rail's foot (Tutorials, Setup, Quit) is below the fold and the rail scrolls
(`before-21-stopped-M`); the stop stays in view. On a phone the rail bar,
capped at 60 % of the height, leaves Setup about 210 px
(`after-13-setup-phone`). The rail foot also wraps raggedly at 248 px
(Tutorials and Setup on one line, Quit alone under them).

### 13. A device page uses a third of a 1080p screen (Proposal)

A device page is one entry 1600 px wide whose controls sit in its left
800 px (`before-10-page-dc-probe-L`); below it is empty sheet. For long
sessions the page could give the readings the room: a reading column at the
focal size on the left and the controls beside it, or a measure of about
75rem so the eye does not travel across empty sheet.

### 14. Smaller items

- Before launch, every device row shows a disabled Hard reset key under a
  "Hard reset" column caption: five dashed ghost keys that say nothing until
  a launch (Proposal: draw the key only on launched rows).
- The skip link ("Skip to the models") goes to the hidden sheet while the
  sign-in screen or Setup is up (Proposal: point it at the open layer).
- A tutorial's card, placed at the top right, covers the entry head's state
  word ("Connection lost") on the device page (`before-31-tutorial-step-L`)
  (Proposal: place it under the head).
- Pressing a rail entry with the mouse draws no focus frame on the entry;
  the heavy frame in some captures is the keyboard path (a scripted click),
  which is intended. Not a defect.

### What holds up well

- The stop is never covered: a click at its centre lands on it at every
  width checked, with Setup, the sign-in screen, the tutorials, the
  acknowledgement and the confirmation open. Ctrl+. works from every layer.
- Contrast: all text clears WCAG AA. Ink on the sheet 13.73, on the rail
  14.82, on a key cap 15.45; muted on the sheet 5.91, on the panel 5.17, on
  DEEP 4.65 (the lowest text pair); trace on the sheet 6.70; white on the
  stop 5.98; signal on the sheet 4.94 (a mark, not text). Control edges: the
  key rim 4.38 and the disabled dashed rim 3.58 both clear the 3:1 non-text
  floor. Text under an overlay's scrim drops to 7.70 (ink) and 3.31 (muted),
  which is the intended "behind" look. A field's face against the sheet is
  only 1.14; the MUTED floor lip is what marks it, and it does.
- Keyboard on the sign-in screen: the email field has focus on arrival, then
  Password, Sign in, Create account..., Proceed as guest, then the skip link,
  the stop and Quit. The stop is two Tab presses from anywhere.
- Targets: rail and tray controls are 44 px, sheet keys 36 px, rail entries
  36 px: above WCAG 2.5.8's 24 px, below 2.5.5's 44 px for every sheet key.

## What this branch changes

All in `src/views/web/static/` (styles.css, and one line of app.js). No
colour of its own, no new token, no server or wire change.

1. `.gate .gate-note` (scoped) instead of a global `.gate-note` rule
   (finding 1).
2. `reserveLogSpace()`: the rail is a column when the stylesheet makes it
   one (`getComputedStyle(rail).flexDirection === 'column'`), not when it is
   taller than wide (finding 2).
3. Setup table: name and status tracks `minmax(min-content, max-content)`,
   names and statuses wrap at spaces, and the stacked table under 62.5rem
   (finding 3).
4. Setup sections: keys at the right edge on every section line, info text
   flexes and wraps, the scan line wraps in place; wrapped values at a 1.35
   line height (finding 4).
5. Entry head: the name's track keeps its longest word; a narrow Overview
   entry (container query, under 32rem) puts the state on its own line
   under the name (finding 6).
6. Sign-in refusal: room kept while empty, warning glyph in ink (finding 9).
7. Rail page list 15 px; table column captions 13/500, engraved
   (finding 10).
8. A unit is hidden beside a word value (finding 11).
9. Three tutorial gamepad steps tightened to the 240-character limit
   (see Tests).

## Proposals not implemented

Ordered by what they would do for a long session at the bench.

1. **Dots that differ by shape, with a legend** (finding 5). Disabled: a
   hollow ring; enabled: a filled dot; error: a filled dot with a "!" (the
   lamp's own error mark). And one mark per row: while every model is
   latched the lamp already says so, so the dot could keep its
   enabled/disabled meaning and leave "stopped" to the lamp. A one-line
   legend under the page list ("● on  ○ off  ! fault") would cost 20 px.
   Needs the owner, and the dot tests change with it.
2. **Emphasis from state in Setup** (finding 7): `go` for Update now only
   when an update is waiting, for Flash only after a check found stale
   boards; neutral otherwise. A schema change in `setup.py`.
3. **No SIM for a model without a simulator** (finding 8), or a row that
   says so and is not launched.
4. **A compact rail for short and narrow screens** (finding 12): the narrow
   stop and a tighter plate under 800 px tall; on a phone the page list
   collapsed behind one key and the alert lines folded to one, so the bar
   stays under about a third of the screen. The rail foot as a two-column
   grid (Tutorials | Setup, Quit under them) so it no longer wraps raggedly.
5. **Device pages that use the screen** (finding 13): readings in a column at
   the focal size, controls beside them, a measure of about 75rem.
6. **A bench scale**: a Setup choice (or the existing `--font-size` flag
   surfaced) that raises the base size one step and the sheet keys to 44 px,
   for gloves and for reading across the bench.
7. **A dim theme** for long sessions in a darkened room: the palette is
   one light set today; a second set of the six tokens, with SIGNAL and
   TRACE re-checked, would be an owner ruling.
8. The smaller items in finding 14: Hard reset only on launched rows, the
   skip link pointed at the open layer, the tutorial card under the head.

## Tests

STATION_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1`, a temporary `TRANSFER_STAGE_DATA_ROOT` and the
headless-Chrome harness, over `test_view_web_client`, `_dashboard`,
`_server`, `_sign_in`, `_tutorial` and `test_wire_golden`:

  on `main` already: two gamepad steps written today (974a0dd, "left stick
  is X, right stick is Y") ran over the 240-character limit (Stepper Probe
  and Chuck Positioner 241, DC Probe 247). The wording is tightened by a
  comma and by "The two triggers drive Z, one each way."; the tutorial file
  then passes (31 passed, 1 xfailed).
- `test_the_slider_keyboard_steps_one_percent_and_ignores_home_and_end` and
  `test_web2_the_picker_draws_on_the_models_still_and_maps_to_source_pixels`
  pass when run alone (load flakes).
- `test_o15_the_device_page_pins_its_head_and_tier_one` fails alone too, and
  fails in the `main` checkout as well; it is on the known-flake list and
  was left alone.
- `test_view_web_client`: 206 passed. `test_wire_golden`: 77 passed.
- One test changed: `test_consecutive_commands_are_one_action_group` pinned
  the `.drawer .section-span > .actions:last-child { flex: 1 0 100% }` rule
  of finding 4, which never matched the Launch row it was written for (its

  the section keys' right edge and that the Launch row's note takes its own
